## Caveats

- **Shaped by case 48.** The Assumptions step was written after reading the baseline's weak
  case-48 keys, so the 48-r1 and 48-r2 gains measure fit to this bench, not generalisation.
  47-r1 and 65-r1 are the fairer read: +0.20 and -0.07 (65-r1 K3 4/5 to 3/5, within run noise).
- **Baseline drift.** Recall compares with the graded 2026-10-31 baseline on CLI `2.1.314`.
  The gate re-ran `baseline@main` on `2.1.316` (the CLI changed every content key); that
  re-run was judged (29/40) but not graded.
- **Label.** The TOMLs say `variant = "baseline"`: that is the suite variant's name on the
  branch. `prompt_sha` (`3d54c89`) identifies the prompt.
- Author-run and author-graded: the same thread wrote the variant and graded it.
